# Vesting Schedule Standardization Notice

## Purpose
To support market stability during the early phase of CEX trading where liquidity is thin, SMARTGOLF performed a vesting schedule standardization for a limited set of early allocations.
Total allocation amounts were not reduced and recipient wallets remained unchanged.

## Effective Date
2026-02-08 (KST)

## Summary of Change
1) Scope  
A limited set of early allocations only.

2) What changed  
The lock and vesting schedule parameters were standardized.

3) What did not change  
● Total allocation amount per recipient was not reduced  
● Recipient wallet addresses remained unchanged
